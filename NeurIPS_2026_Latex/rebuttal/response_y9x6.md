# Response to Reviewer y9x6

Three new experiments since submission. (1) End-to-end State-Channel matching under observation noise, same 800 train / 200 val tasks: clean+exact 47.5%, 30% noise+exact **11.0%**, 30% noise+soft **54.5%**, no State Channel 31.0%. (2) A success-conditioned probe on DR3's own features. (3) A like-for-like seed replicate of 1.5B-WebShop that **does not reproduce** the submitted number; reported below.

**Generalisation to noisier, open-ended, partially observable environments.** Your concern is right, in a sharper form than posed: a *failed* matcher is actively harmful, not merely uninformative. 30% observation noise with exact hashing drops state coverage 0.590→0.178 and success to 11.0%, *below* the 31.0% from deleting the State Channel. The dependence lives in the lookup operator, not the method: the same progress map with a TF-IDF matcher (one config field, no new dependency) gives coverage 0.654 and 54.5%. We read 54.5 vs 47.5 as "at least as good", not "better" — our three-seed spread on a comparable cell has sd 4.9pp. Noise is added only to *the matcher's copy* of the observation, so task difficulty is fixed. Missing cell you would rightly demand: clean observations + soft matcher, separating "soft matching repairs noise" from "soft matching is simply the better matcher"; it is running. Honest cost: Φ is ~3 lines of per-environment code on ALFWorld and ~210 lines of hand-written domain logic on WebShop. The interface transfers; someone still authors the potential. If Φ cannot be built, ALFWorld degrades rather than breaks (SC off: 31.0 vs CHORD 27.0, McNemar p=0.31, i.e. parity) but WebShop does not (1.0 vs CHORD 11.5). There SC is load-bearing, and we say so.

**Multi-seed robustness of the reported gains.** Partly no. ALFWorld 1.5B at the submitted configuration, curriculum pinned: 47.5 / 41.0 / 50.5 at val@100 (seed spread ≈5pp), every seed improving over the second half (+4.5 to +5.0). We withdraw an earlier figure of 'sd 4.9pp' for a −DR3 seed set: those runs predate our `data.task_seed` fix, so each re-drew its own 800 tasks — pairwise overlap 264/269/271 of 800, statistically indistinguishable from independent draws — making 4.9pp an upper bound that confounds seed with a two-thirds-different curriculum, not an estimate of seed variance. 1.5B-WebShop reproduces only partially, and we report the full set. Against the submitted **36.0%**, four replicates of the identical configuration (verified field-by-field against the executed config backups) give: **4.0%** and **2.5%** when the run seed is allowed to re-draw the 800-task curriculum, and **15.0%** and **24.5%** when the curriculum is pinned to the paper's own task draw. The separation is the point: in this codebase `data.seed` sets both run randomness *and* which 800 tasks are selected, so the two low numbers confound seed with a two-thirds change of training curriculum. We have added `data.task_seed` to decouple them. The honest statement is that the submitted number sits at the top of a distribution whose pinned-curriculum replicates run 15.0–24.5%, not that it reproduces. Mechanism, a property of the cell: strict success needs every requested attribute clicked, 88.4% of training tasks request ≥2, and all runs first converge on a 3-action policy that earns partial credit but is structurally incapable of an exact match (0 successes in 10,735 episodes with fewer clicks than required); escaping it is a late stochastic event — step 74 in the paper run, 81 in one replicate, never in the other. We will report the spread and never a best-seed number; a 150-step run is executing.

**Principled density-ratio estimator, or learned heuristic weighting.** Heuristic — a variance-controlled replay weight — and we correct the paper. The applied weight is the bounded α-relative ratio, ŵ ≤ 1.13, so DR3 can only ever *down-weight* teacher samples; teacher samples receive exactly **one** correction (the missing behaviour log-probability is imputed), not a product of two ratios. Eqs. 8 and 9 are corrected and "principled estimator" becomes "bounded surrogate". Your confound is testable, so we tested it: probes on DR3's own reward-free features do not weaken when the student side is restricted to successes (D_succ 90.0% over all steps, 98.2% early), and late in training successful vs failed student rollouts receive P(student) 0.861 vs 0.869, against 0.280 for teacher trajectories. Our own caveat: this is an offline probe on a fixed policy snapshot, not the online discriminator.

**Separation and correction, or the additional imitation signal.** Separable, and we ran the isolating ablation. Removing BC entirely: 34.0 (ALFWorld) / 16.5 (WebShop) with *no imitation term at all*, against full DUET 47.5/36.0 and GRPO 1.0/0.5. Conversely, keeping BC and removing DR3 costs 26.5pp on WebShop, and on ALFWorld it costs 14.0pp at the midpoint (42.5% vs 28.5% at step 50) — DR3 buys a much faster approach to the plateau, which an endpoint-only snapshot hides. More broadly, across the two evaluation points our protocol already produces, DUET goes 42.5 → 47.5 while CHORD goes 30.0 → 27.0, LUFFY 26.0 → 5.5 and GRPO 16.5 → 1.0: **every method without our corrections degrades over the second half of training; DUET is the only one still improving at the end.**

**Cache size, diversity, quality, success filtering.** ALFWorld: 19,497 successful demos over 2,348 tasks (8.3/task; sampled 72B, success-filtered, 80.6% kept). WebShop: 26,178 over 5,691 tasks (verified gold sequences). Diversity: 4.4 distinct action sequences out of 8.5 per task, 7.6% single-path. The cache-size ablation is **not run** (10%/1% caches in progress); we offer no prediction in its place. Teacher quality as you posed it is also not run — a weaker-teacher cache is being collected. What we can show varies the *student*, not the teacher: with the cache fixed, the teacher–student reward gap is 0.93/0.55 (early/late) at 1.5B vs 0.42/0.06 at 7B, and DR3's teacher gradient share falls 0.241→0.079. In that teacher-no-longer-better regime DUET reaches 86.5 vs GRPO 85.0, while LUFFY falls to 82.5 — *below* the no-teacher baseline. Mixing-ratio sensitivity: a 2-per-group cell is queued.

**Simpler reward-shaping baselines, and alternative off-policy corrections.** No shaping control exists yet, and we will not offer a diagnostic instead. Two matched-magnitude controls are running and will be reported whichever way they land: a *shuffled* progress map (coverage and mean bonus held fixed, corr(position, Φ) 0.77→0.05) and a task-agnostic step-count/novelty potential. Exact importance sampling is unavailable by construction — no likelihoods in the cache, no shared tokenizer — so the revision positions DR3 against advantage-weighting (AWAC/IQL) and truncation (V-trace). The two controlled points we have are uncorrected replay (−DR3: 47.5/9.5) and LUFFY's shaping as an alternative weighting, net-negative at 7B.
