Question 01
Both use teacher trajectories but no teacher logits. Yet CHORD gains 5.4 pp over native DUET. What is the mechanism?
Answer: the per-token gradient coefficient. DR3 multiplies by p(1−p) and vanishes on rare tokens. BC multiplies by (1−p) and stays active.
Q1 · Gradient Geometry

Same data, different calculus

CHORD · Behavior Cloning
$L_\text{BC} = -\mu \cdot \log \pi_\theta(a^*|s)$
$\Delta z(a^*) = \mu \cdot (1 - \pi_\theta)$
  • μ is a per-token, unit coefficient.
  • When p is small, (1−p) ≈ 1 — gradient is largest on surprising tokens.
  • Sign is unconditionally positive (push a* up).
DUET · DR3 Teacher Surrogate
$L_\text{DR3} = -A(\tau) \cdot \text{clip}(w_\text{hat} \cdot \pi_\theta)$
$\Delta z(a^*) = A \cdot w_\text{hat} \cdot \pi_\theta(1-\pi_\theta)$
  • A(τ)·w_hat is a sequence-level scalar.
  • When p is small, p(1−p) ≈ 0 — gradient vanishes on rare tokens.
  • Sign depends on advantage A(τ) (conditional).

Empirical check: actor/off_pg_cliphit_rate = 0.000 throughout training. The suppression is not from PPO clipping — it is the p(1−p) factor in the softmax geometry.

Q2 · The v25 Incident

μ_valley = 0.05 also protects syntax

v25: remove BC entirely, widen PPO clip (0.6 → 2.0). What happened:

Training trajectory
  • Steps 1–97: normal, reward rising to 0.62
  • Step 98: single toxic gradient, response_length 2053 → 1080
  • Step 100: Val = −0.041, grad-norm = 48.4
Failure mode (200 rollouts)
  • 47% — doubled <action> opening tags
  • 69% — hallucinated wrappers (<search>, <story>, <when>)
  • Extreme: recursive <when result="..."> XML trees

Without BC persistently anchoring low-probability grammar tokens like <action>, PPO gradually drifts their logits. Once syntax breaks, reward collapses. v24's μ_valley = 0.05 is a permanent anchor against this drift.

Question 03
Can we reintegrate BC into the dual-channel story without adding a third channel?
Answer: treat the Action Channel as a curriculum of two complementary gradient operators, not a single loss.
Q3 · Unified Narrative

Teacher-gradient curriculum

Action Channel · on teacher trajectories
$L_\text{action} = \mu_t \cdot L_\text{BC} + L_\text{DR3}$
  • μ_t : 0.3 → 0.05 over 25 steps — the curriculum schedule.
  • L_BC dominates early: teacher-token identity imprint, (1−p) gradient.
  • L_DR3 dominates late: trajectory-level credit assignment, with w_hat → 1 natural fade-out.
  • μ_valley = 0.05 maintains a format-token anchor indefinitely.
State Channel · on on-policy trajectories · unchanged
$L_\text{SC} = \beta \cdot \Phi(\tau) \cdot \nabla \log \pi_\theta$

Novelty claim: density-ratio-driven automatic curriculum. CHORD uses a hand-tuned μ schedule with no trajectory credit; DUET's DR3 provides automatic fade and advantage-weighted teacher correction. v22 / v23 (constant μ) empirically fail, showing the schedule is load-bearing.

Q3 · Reviewer Anticipation

How we defend it

Likely questionOur response
R1 · Isn't this just CHORD + DR3? CHORD has no trajectory-level credit on teacher samples and no automatic fade. v22/v23 (constant μ) empirically fail — the decaying schedule is load-bearing.
R2 · If BC is needed, is SC still necessary? BC addresses token identity; SC addresses state-visitation credit. v4 (SC off) collapses to 0.343 — hard evidence.
R3 · WebShop-specific hack? BC value ∝ rare-token gap × format fragility. ALFWorld prediction: gain ≈ 0. To validate: v24 on ALFWorld 1.5B.
R4 · What about AWAC as a single-operator baseline? Honest disclosure: not run. Claim downgrades to "two operators with independent analyses." Listed as future work.

Five things to take away

  1. DUET wins on 5 of 6 env×scale cells; the one loss (WebShop 1.5B, CHORD 0.603 vs DUET-v1 0.549) led to the ablation study.
  2. v24 = DUET + decaying BC reaches 0.678 — beating CHORD by +7.5 pp and DUET-v1 by +12.9 pp.
